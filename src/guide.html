How to Play

GeoShuffle is a slide puzzle game. Your goal is to reconstruct scrambled satellite images of famous locations and landmarks around the Earth.

  • Tap or click on any tile next to the empty space to slide it into that space.
  • Rearrange the tiles until the satellite image looks correct.
  • Once you solve it, you will see details about the location and a link to view it on Google Maps!

How to Move Tiles

You can slide tiles around using clicks, keyboard buttons, or swipe gestures:

Keyboard Buttons

W or ↑ Slide tile up
S or ↓ Slide tile down
A or ← Slide tile left
D or → Slide tile right

Mobile Swipes

On mobile or tablet screens, simply swipe your finger across the puzzle grid in any direction to slide tiles into the gap.

How to Change Nickname

You can personalize your handle name and adjust helper layouts:

  • First-time Load: On your first visit, a welcome prompt will ask you to enter a nickname.
  • Change Name: Open Settings, click on the Player Name field, type a nickname, and it will be saved.
  • Helper Numbers: Toggle "Number Assist" in Settings to show helper numbers on tiles.
  • Grid Outline: Hidden and shown with the same "Number Assist" toggle.

How to Play with Friends

Compete head-to-head with other players in real-time:

  • Host a Match: Click "Host Lobby", copy the 6-letter room code, and send it to your friend.
  • Join a Friend: Enter your friend's room code in the input box and click "Join Lobby".
  • Quick Play: Click "Quick Match" to search for and pair with any random player currently online.
  • Who Wins?: The first player to successfully solve the map wins! You can see your opponent's move count live at the top of your screen.

Ranks & Scorecards

Track your progress and share your puzzle achievements:

Global Leaderboard

When you solve a puzzle, your score is automatically sent to the global leaderboard. Check the top ranks to see how fast you are compared to players globally!

Personal Runs

Your last 100 solved runs are saved locally. Review your own solve history, dates, times, and move counts in the Local leaderboard tab.

Download & Share

Once solved, you can click "Download Scorecard" to save a beautiful picture card of your completion, or click "Share Score" to post it on social media.
